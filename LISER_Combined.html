Explain the cycle clockwise. Reproducibility and transparency apply to every stage. The dashed return arrow carries experience from the decision back into the next question. These engineering practices describe the proposed workflow, not a claim that every tool is already implemented in the linked repository.

  • Code will not run elsewhere: use a Docker image and pin dependencies with renv or Nix.
  • Results shift between runs: set random seeds in code and use a targets dependency graph to manage reruns. Reproducibility also requires stable inputs, environments and deterministic computations; a DAG alone does not guarantee identical results.
  • Output does not match the exhibit: have the pipeline generate the tables and figures consumed by Quarto.
  • Definitions drift between years: use unit-tested, versioned indicator functions with a change log.
  • Nobody notices a failure: run continuous integration on every commit to rebuild, test and flag failures.

The repository link gives the audience a route to inspect this presentation. Allow about one minute.

Appendix / Methods and limitations

How to read the results

Method or limitation What it means for interpretation
Illustrative rental scenario Commune mean €/m² × chosen area. Advertised, not contracted rents; no adjustment for dwelling quality or size. Small flats can have different €/m².
Constant purchasing power Historical budget = 2025 budget × IPCN(year) / IPCN(2025). €1,200 becomes about €876 in 2010. No earnings adjustment; classify using unrounded values. The nominal toggle is a different comparison.
Incomplete coverage This workbook withholds rates below 30 advertisements. In 2025, 34 of 100 communes have rates; hatching means unavailable, not no housing. Do not generalise this threshold to other Observatoire products.
Matched geography and sample Predecessor rates are offer-weighted only when every component is observed. The baseline 29 → 11 result concerns 29 communes observed in both 2010 and 2025; listing composition can still change.
Counts are not housing access Unweighted commune counts are not counts of people, homes or vacancies. No confidence intervals or quality-adjusted rent index. Deposits, charges, eligibility, transport and shared housing are not modelled.
No departure or causal estimate Eurostat’s departure-age indicator is national; the census is a 2021 snapshot. Neither provides a municipal departure series. The map does not show that rents caused delayed departures. €1,200 and 45 m² are chosen assumptions, not measured young-adult means.
Local action remains a proposal Schifflange’s 246 is a 2022 stock, excluding GLS, emergency housing and assisted ownership—not current vacancies or youth provision. A local study is needed before selecting an intervention.

The reproducibility study’s 86% includes missing materials; it does not mean 86% of conclusions were disproved. Engineering safeguards shown are proposed practices, not all implemented in this repository.

Use this page to answer methodological questions. The 29-to-11 statement is the default €1,200 in 2025 purchasing power and 45 m² scenario, even if controls were changed earlier. The matched sample is not representative of all communes; no national young-adult affordability or housing-availability estimate follows.
